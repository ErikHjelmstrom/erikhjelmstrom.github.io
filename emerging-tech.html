Emerging Tech for Creatives.

A seven module learning journey exploring how emerging technologies can shape creative work, businesses and human experiences.

Format
Online learning program
Structure
Seven modules
Focus
Creativity · Technology · Strategy

THE PROGRAM

Building an evolving creative toolkit.

Emerging Tech for Creatives combined creative practice with new technologies across seven connected modules.

The program moved from collaboration and storytelling to branding, AI-assisted workflows, user centred design, immersive experiences and responsible technology.

The journey below presents the complete program. I have selected the projects that best represent my work for deeper case studies.

THE LEARNING JOURNEY

Seven modules.
One evolving toolkit.

Select a module

01 / FOUNDATION

Introduction

A foundation in the Hyper Island way of learning, focused on trust, collaboration, reflection and self-leadership.

Through the Way Course and reflective exercises, we explored how psychological safety, communication and personal responsibility can support both individual development and stronger teamwork.

  • Self-leadership
  • Reflection
  • Team collaboration
  • Psychological safety

02 / SELECTED CASE

Digital Storytelling for Creatives

Exploring storytelling as a tool for creative expression, communication and audience engagement across digital platforms.

The module combined text, imagery, video and emerging AI tools to develop original narratives for different audiences and formats.

  • Digital storytelling
  • Creative concepts
  • Audience engagement
  • AI-supported creation

Selected project · Case study in progress

03 / SELECTED CASE

Building and Marketing a Creative Brand

Connecting brand identity and storytelling with entrepreneurship, digital marketing and audience growth.

The module focused on shaping a clear creative brand, communicating its purpose and using practical marketing tools to reach and engage the right audience.

  • Brand strategy
  • Brand storytelling
  • Digital marketing
  • Audience growth

Selected project · Case study in progress

04 / CREATIVE TECHNOLOGY

AI Tools and Workflows

A practical introduction to integrating AI tools into creative workflows.

We explored how AI can support the generation and refinement of text, visual content and audio, with an emphasis on using the tools intentionally rather than treating them as the creative outcome.

  • AI tools
  • Creative workflows
  • Text and images
  • Audio experimentation

05 / DESIGN

User Centered Design Principles

Designing accessible and engaging solutions around the needs of real users.

Through design thinking, prototyping and user testing, we explored how feedback can be integrated into the creative process and used to improve a solution.

  • Design thinking
  • Prototyping
  • User feedback
  • User testing

06 / IMMERSIVE MEDIA

XR and Immersive Experiences for Creatives

An introduction to virtual and augmented reality, interactivity and immersive creative experiences.

The module moved from experiencing immersive work and paper prototyping to interactive concepts and practical experimentation with tools such as Unity.

  • XR
  • Paper prototyping
  • Interactivity
  • Unity

07 / RESPONSIBLE TECHNOLOGY

Ethics and Sustainability in Creative Technologies

Exploring the ethical and sustainable use of technology within creative industries.

Through case studies and reflection, the module examined intellectual property, environmental impact and the responsible use of AI.

  • Responsible AI
  • Intellectual property
  • Sustainability
  • Ethical decisions

WHAT I TAKE FORWARD

Technology matters when it makes an idea more useful, meaningful or memorable.

The program expanded my creative toolkit, but also taught me to question when technology genuinely strengthens an experience and when it simply adds complexity.
